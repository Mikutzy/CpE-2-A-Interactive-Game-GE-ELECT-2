HOW TO PLAY
01 / DRAWGet one scenario card about gender expectations.
02 / ALLOCATESpend all five tokens across four social costs.
03 / CHANGEChoose a response and receive your personal receipt.

There are no right or wrong token distributions. This game is about noticing how gender stereotypes can affect people’s choices and opportunities.

ROUND 01 · DRAW A SCENARIO

Pick a card.

Five situation cards, face down. Choose one at random and see what society is charging.

Your situation is...

Read the situation. What gender expectation is at work, and what might it cost this person?

THINK ABOUT IT

ROUND 02 · PAY THE SOCIAL COST

Where does the cost land?

You have 5 fictional tokens. Use + and − to decide how strongly each cost affects the person in your scenario. Put all five on one cost or spread them out. You must place all five to continue.

ROUND 03 · MAKE A CHANGE

What should change?

Who is paying this cost, and what could make it fairer? Choose the response you believe would help most.

ROUND 04 · YOUR TAKEAWAY

Your receipt is ready.

A small record of the social cost you noticed and the change you want to see.
